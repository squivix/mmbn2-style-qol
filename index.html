BN2 Style Change QoL Patcher

For Mega Man Battle Network 2 (GBA), USA or Europe. Everything runs in your browser; your ROM never leaves your computer.

1Your ROM

2Changes

Style Change every battles

The original game needs 280. Any number from 1 to 255.

At a Style Change MegaMan asks which element you want, offering the ones the new type doesn't have yet. The type is still decided by how you fight.

No more overwriting: all 16 elemental styles plus Normal and Hub, paged in the MegaMan screen. Without "Pick the element", the element is random among the missing ones.

A small number in the MegaMan screen's title bar counts down to the next Style Change. No style points or hints about the next style.

Patch a copy of your ROM. Saves carry over. With "Keep every style", don't load a save holding more than three styles in the unpatched game.

Needs the clean ROM: USA (AE2E) CRC32 6D961F82 or Europe (AM2P) CRC32 66341F3B. The Legacy Collection is not supported.

Prefer a regular patcher? The .bps and .ips files are on the GitHub page.
